    - Campfire Embers = $6 - uncommon - gains x0.2 mult at the end of round (Not boss rounds)
    - Potluck = $7 - uncommon - fill consumable slots with the fool after boss round is defeated (Must have room)
    - Fresh Trail = $5 - common - gains +5 chips if played hand has not already been played this round
    - Silver Reserve = $8 - rare - x0.4mult for every $25 you have
    - Roulette Wheel = $5 - uncommon - randomly gives x2.5, x1.5, x1.5 mult each day

    - Supply Caravan = $4 - common - +16 miles for each equipment card owned
    - Split Trail = $7 - uncommon - x2.5 mult if played hand contains a scoring odd and even dice value
